Project Purpose & Institutional Objectives

Why Climate-Aware Credit Risk?

Traditional credit scoring models rely heavily on historical financial collateral and past repayment records. However, increasing climate volatility—such as unseasonal monsoons, heatwaves, and prolonged droughts—directly impacts crop yields and farmer repayment capacities.

This system aims to bridge agricultural finance with climate intelligence, providing NABARD and institutional lenders with a transparent, climate-adjusted risk evaluation model.

Key System Capabilities (Roadmap)

  • Loan Record Management: Centralized dataset containing farmer demographics, loan amounts, and crop choices.
  • Agronomic & Climate Telemetry: Integration of temperature, rainfall deficit, and satellite vegetation indices.
  • Machine Learning Risk Engine: Predictive yield modeling, default probability (PD), and expected loss (EL).
  • Explainable AI (SHAP): Transparent factor-by-factor risk breakdowns for credit officers.
